How it was made

For each minute, every 30 m cell of the elevation model looks toward the sun. If the terrain along that line rises above it, the cell is in shadow. The sun's position is calculated for Parque Central.

Hatched cells are sunlit inside the data, but their line to the sun leaves the elevation model low enough that mountains outside it could still shade them. Terrain outside the data is assumed to reach no higher than m.
